# Atlas Balance Product Context

## Register

Product UI.

## Users

- Finance owners, treasury managers, administrators and operators who need to read balances, review movements, import bank statements, manage permissions and prepare safe exports.
- Small internal teams, typically local/on-prem Windows deployments, where trust, speed and traceability matter more than novelty.
- Power users who repeat table, import, review and admin flows many times per week.

## Product Purpose

Atlas Balance is a local financial control product for treasury visibility: balances, accounts, holders, movements, imports, review, alerts, permissions, backups, audit and AI-assisted analysis.

The product wins when a user can answer these questions quickly and confidently:

- How much money is available, immobilized or at risk?
- Which holder, account, bank, country or currency explains a change?
- Which movements need review, correction or escalation?
- Who can see or change each financial surface?
- Is the local installation safe enough to update, backup and operate?

## Brand Personality

- Precise.
- Serious.
- Premium without noise.
- Calm under operational pressure.
- Direct, like a competent financial analyst, not a marketing site.

## Anti-References

- Crypto dashboard.
- Generic SaaS landing page.
- Purple AI gradient product.
- Decorative glassmorphism.
- Card wall with every metric at the same weight.
- Playful admin UI for destructive actions.
- Ambiguous copy around money, permissions, backups or updates.

## Design Principles

1. Treasury first. Numbers, risks, permissions and movements get priority over decoration.
2. Hierarchy before color. Size, spacing, grouping and alignment must do the heavy work.
3. Dense, not cramped. A financial cockpit can be information-rich without becoming noisy.
4. One system. Tokens, controls, charts, tables, forms and overlays must feel like the same product.
5. State clarity beats delight. Empty, loading, error, permission, stale, offline and partial-data states must be explicit.
6. Mobile is operational, not secondary. Critical flows must degrade into usable review and action patterns, not decorative stacks.
7. Motion confirms state. It should never slow repeated work or hide financial changes.

## Accessibility And Inclusion

- WCAG AA contrast for text, controls, focus rings and chart/state colors.
- Keyboard access for shell, nav, forms, modals, tables, date/select controls and floating chat.
- Visible focus and deterministic tab order.
- Reduced motion support for all nonessential animation.
- Touch targets fit real mobile use.
- Color is never the only carrier of success, warning, danger or selection.
- Long Spanish labels, currency names, bank names, paths, emails, IBANs and localized numbers must not break layout.
